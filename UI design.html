Diode Current Equation

The ideal diode equation models the current flowing through a p-n junction diode as a function of voltage applied:

I_D = I_S * (e^(V_D / (n*V_T)) - 1)

Where I_S is saturation current, V_T is thermal voltage (26mV at room temp), and n is ideality factor.

Physical Address = (CS × 16) + IP
